# Privacy Policy — Weld Consumable Calculator (Varyos Weld)

_Last updated: 2026-09-06_

Weld Consumable Calculator ("the App") is developed to help welding engineers, estimators, and workshop planners calculate weld volume, filler consumption, and related planning metrics.

## Summary

Varyos Weld is primarily a local, on-device calculator. Account registration is optional and only involves the data described below — we do not use any analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs.

## What data the App stores

- **Presets and saved calculations**: joint presets you save (dimensions, process settings, deposition rates) are stored locally on your device by default. If you register with an email, your saved calculations are also synced to a cloud backend (a Google Apps Script / Google Sheet) so the same account can retrieve them on another device.
- **Registration data**: if you choose to register, your name and email are collected. Your name is kept local to your device only and is never transmitted anywhere. Your email is submitted to a Google Form (so we can send a welcome email) and is also used as the identifier for your cloud-synced saved calculations.
- **PDF reports**: reports you generate are created and shared directly from your device using the native share/print sheet. Nothing is uploaded to a server as part of generating a report.
- **Subscription/purchase data**: in-app purchases (Premium PDF export) are processed by RevenueCat, which handles subscription and purchase data according to its own standard practices. We do not receive your payment details.

## What the App does not do

- Account creation is optional — the App is fully usable as a guest, with all data staying on your device.
- No analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs.
- No location, contacts, camera, or microphone access.

## Third-party services

The App uses a Google Apps Script / Google Sheet backend for optional account-based preset sync, a Google Form for registration/welcome emails, and RevenueCat for subscription management. It does not integrate any ad networks, crash reporting, or analytics SDKs.

## Account deletion

You can delete your account at any time from the Account screen in the App. We delete your local saved calculations immediately and attempt to delete your cloud-synced calculations; if cloud deletion doesn't fully complete, the App will tell you and you can retry or contact us. Your registered email is removed from our systems as well; because this specific step relies on a Google Form response and has no automated delete-by-value mechanism, it is handled manually and completed within 30 days of your request. Your name, if you entered one, was never transmitted or stored server-side in the first place (see "Registration data" above), so there is nothing server-side to delete for it.

## Children's Privacy

The App is a professional engineering tool not directed at children.

## Changes to this policy

If the App's data practices change in the future, this policy will be updated and the change will be reflected in the App Store listing.
