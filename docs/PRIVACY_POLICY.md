# Privacy Policy — Weld Consumable Calculator

_Last updated: 2026-07-10_

Weld Consumable Calculator ("the App") is developed to help welding engineers, estimators, and workshop planners calculate weld volume, filler consumption, and related planning metrics.

## Summary

**The App does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.** All calculations and data storage happen entirely on your device.

## What data the App stores

- **Presets**: Any joint presets you save (dimensions, process settings, deposition rates) are stored locally on your device using the operating system's standard local storage. This data never leaves your device.
- **PDF reports**: Reports you generate are created and saved/shared directly from your device using the native share/print sheet. The App does not upload report content to any server.

## What the App does not do

- No account creation or sign-in is required.
- No analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs are integrated.
- No data is transmitted to the developer or any third party.
- No location, contacts, camera, or microphone access is requested.

## Third-party services

The App does not integrate any third-party services that collect data (no ad networks, no crash reporting, no analytics).

## Children's Privacy

The App is a professional engineering tool not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

If the App's data practices change in the future (for example, if analytics or cloud sync are added), this policy will be updated and the change will be reflected in the App Store listing.
