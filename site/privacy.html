Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-10

Weld Consumable Calculator does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. All calculations and data storage happen entirely on your device.

What data the app stores

  • Presets — joint presets you save (dimensions, process settings, deposition rates) are stored locally on your device. This data never leaves your device.
  • PDF reports — reports you generate are created and shared directly from your device using the native share/print sheet. Nothing is uploaded to a server.

What the app does not do

  • No account creation or sign-in required.
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs.
  • No data transmitted to the developer or any third party.
  • No location, contacts, camera, or microphone access.

Third-party services

The app does not integrate any third-party services that collect data (no ad networks, no crash reporting, no analytics).

Children's Privacy

The app is a professional engineering tool not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change in the future, this policy will be updated and the App Store listing will reflect the change.
